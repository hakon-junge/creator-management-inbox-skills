# Module: affiliate (commission on referred sales)

On by the pack default, `pricing: commission` (mode `lead`) or `creator_types: affiliates`;
off when `affiliate.md` says `none`. Terms: the user's `affiliate.md`, the single source of
truth, quoted exactly, never from memory or an old email. Loads on its trigger (always in
mode `lead` or with `budget: no-cash`); unloaded, the core's never-spend-a-lever rule keeps
it out of our first number. It beats the core only through a declared exception below.

## The affiliate lever (the last lever in the stack)

<!-- rule:aff-entry -->
**Never in our first number** - not in an opening offer, not in our first counter. It
enters at their first pushback, as the trade that lets you hold or lower the fee, or as
the exit rung when no fee lands. Leading with it trains them to treat it as baseline and
burns its pull. When it enters, it enters at `{{AFFILIATE_RATE_STANDARD}}`;
`{{AFFILIATE_RATE_LAUNCH}}` is held back as the trade for a flat-fee push ("if you're
looking for more, I can bring the affiliate up") - never open at the launch rate. ("Lock
in the launch rate by joining now" is for affiliate-only, non-negotiation contexts.)
**Declared exceptions, and only these:** mode `lead` (`pricing: commission` - the
affiliate is the opener and a flat fee is a rare, flagged exception); Track A of the
net-new ladder (`views-pricing`); an established creator's collaborative re-book or
restructure (`budget-always-on`, and G.2 below), where the whole package goes on the
table at once.

**On a recalibration of an established creator the affiliate UPGRADE is a standard
structural sweetener:** push to `{{AFFILIATE_RATE_LAUNCH}}` as part of the restructured
deal, and for trusted long-term partners the upside may be NAMED conditionally - "if
performance grows, there's a path up to `{{AFFILIATE_RATE_VIP}}`" - always conditional,
never a promise, and never with a permanence claim attached (check the program terms; the
VIP tier may still be a future phase, and exact terms stay in `affiliate.md`).

**Who already holds which rate.** Before writing any commission figure, check who already
holds or has already been told which rate (recorded in the creator's note). A creator with
no deal and no live post is net-new by the ledger but may already hold
`{{AFFILIATE_RATE_LAUNCH}}` on the platform, and opening them at
`{{AFFILIATE_RATE_STANDARD}}` is a visible cut on a rate they can see in their own dashboard.
Ledger picks the track; the roster vetoes the number.

**Exit rung.** Where no rate lands, the parting still carries a standalone
**`{{AFFILIATE_RATE_LAUNCH}}` affiliate**, no deliverable attached, usable on any surface
they already own. Respectful decline first, door open, this alongside as pure upside - not a
salvage attempt. Zero cost to us, and it is the same move run on creator-owned surfaces
(Scenario N, the retainer variant). The engine's `AFFILIATE-ONLY` verdict routes here, or
to G.2 for an established creator.

**Radical downside disclosure** - volunteer the weak spots of the offer before they find
them: "I am not going to promise you a number... roughly one month in five lands under
`{{PAYOUT_THRESHOLD}}`, and refunds get deducted." Disclosed unasked, that deal still
closed. The affiliate pitch that admits its downside is more credible than the one that
doesn't.

**Written commitments** (value lever 6) add the affiliate terms: percentage and window
pinned exactly.

## Creator-owned channels are never paid line items

The "for now" doctrine: email lists, nurture sequences, DM funnels, retainers on their own
assets. The affiliate is the monetization path for creator-owned surfaces - it pays exactly
when their asset converts, and it compounds as the asset grows. A one-time co-build fee
for an asset WE will actively use can be a deliberate exception; a recurring fee on their
own channel never is. A silently omitted line (architecture) keeps pulling them back
toward the path we DO offer - usually the affiliate; expect them to reopen it once, and one
soft affiliate-pointer is the maximum response, never a price.

- **N - Multi-line system proposal from an established creator (the cherry-pick).** They
  pitch a four or five line "system" - short-form + long-form + email sequence + newsletter +
  retainer, each with its own fee, usually wrapped in a genuinely smart funnel narrative. Do
  NOT negotiate it line by line (that's defending five fronts on their battlefield).
  Architecture first, then: (1) **echo-accept** the line whose price-to-scope already favors
  us, restated in their own words, their bundled sweeteners locked in as scope; (2)
  **house-number** the lines we want but on our terms (the newsletter at our tested rate, not
  theirs); (3) **silently omit** what we won't buy - creator-owned-channel fees above all; (4)
  **micro-spec** inside the echo; (5) affiliate stays the structure layer on top, not a new
  concession; (6) chalant posture, one light reason ("controlled testing of the new channel
  first before scaling"), onboard-close; (7) "we can layer more in as we go" keeps every
  future add OUR lever. (Worked case: Sasha Bell, five lines in - one echo-accepted line +
  one house number + full silence on the sequence.)
  **Creator-owned retainer variant:** an established partner of several years pitched a
  five-figure monthly "community package" retainer (a fixed number of dedicated posts,
  community posts and shorts per month on a multi-month contract, with a per-extra-dedicated
  add-on) for placement on their OWN community. The reply kept the giveaway logic worth
  keeping, declined the recurring fee plainly ("we deliberately do not pay recurring fees for
  placement on a creator's own community... not a comment on the value you deliver, just a
  different model to ours"), and re-pointed the creator-owned surface to the affiliate as its
  monetization path (cost-per-signup logic works in their favour, and it compounds as the
  community grows, which a fixed monthly fee never does), with a no-fault exit and the door
  left open.

## End of deal

- **G.2 - The reduced/held-flat + affiliate restructure (the established continuation
  move).** Continue a proven relationship at a lower or held flat with the affiliate as part
  of the STRUCTURE. (Marla Chen: four videos at the recalibrated rate plus
  `{{AFFILIATE_RATE_STANDARD}}`. Sasha Bell: one more post at a small flat plus commission,
  confirmed committed.) A declared exception to `aff-entry`: affiliate-as-core-structure is
  fine in an ESTABLISHED post-collab restructure.

- **Renewals** (money-core §2): a pinned affiliate with no flat rate re-onboards on
  commission, a flat base only as a fallback; **never flat-fee-renew a pinned affiliate** -
  paying a flat fee to someone already earning commission double-pays for the same
  audience. On a cut or a do-not-re-onboard, the affiliate track is the no-fee path.

## Program answers this module carries

- `pricing: commission` - "we work on commission: [terms from `affiliate.md`]"; no engine.
- `budget: no-cash` (outside ecommerce) - commission is the offer the core's no-cash
  sentence leads into.
- `creator_types: affiliates` - commission terms from `affiliate.md`, no flat fee.

## Never (this module)

- Never promise permanence ("for life") the terms don't state.
- Never put the affiliate in our first number (`aff-entry`).
- Never pay recurring fees for creator-owned channels (email lists, sequences, DM funnels,
  retainers) - the affiliate is the monetization path for their own assets; a one-time
  co-build for an asset WE actively use is the only deliberate exception.
