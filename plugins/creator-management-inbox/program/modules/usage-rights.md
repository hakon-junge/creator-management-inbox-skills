# Module: usage-rights (boosting, whitelisting, paid usage of creator content)

On by the pack default; mode `included-first-deal` (rights ride inside a first deal's fee;
windows and extension prices in `{{BOOSTING_TERMS}}`) or `priced` (every right its own
priced line from `{{USAGE_RIGHTS_RATES}}`). Sections marked *included-first-deal* or
*priced* apply only in that mode; in `priced` mode rights are never "included".

## The lever (after timing in the stack)

**Boosting / whitelisting rights.** On NEW creators, always retained (see "no benchmark
yet") - not a chip. *Included-first-deal:* **net-new cold: retained in every case, and
REQUESTED explicitly wherever cash is on the table - the shorter default window on a flat
fee, the longer one on the creative-support close (both in `{{BOOSTING_TERMS}}`). On an
affiliate-only deal it stays retained but unasked: no fee to attach it to.** Every dollar
of cash buys boosting rights - no exceptions. On ESTABLISHED creators it becomes
tradeable: offer to drop it, or run it at the per-30-day rate in `{{BOOSTING_TERMS}}`.
A launch that needs one exact audience buys reach via boosting (or the affiliate), never
an above-ceiling fee.

## Boosting on new creators - the "no benchmark yet" line (*included-first-deal*)

On any first collaboration always keep boosting rights, and explain WHY in the email:

> "Since this is our first collaboration, we don't have a benchmark yet - so this is really a
> trust-and-exploration phase to see how we grow together. To do that properly I need to see
> two layers: how your content performs organically, and how it performs with paid behind it.
> That's what tells us what a blended return looks like with you specifically."

The logic (adapt, don't recite): some creators convert well boosted without the strongest
audience, some are strong both ways, some are great organic but don't respond to paid -
you can't know which without both layers. Rights are how you learn the creator, not
something you're extracting.

**Disclose the scope ceiling in writing, unasked**, exactly as `{{BOOSTING_TERMS}}` sets it
(which video, whose handle, re-cuts, the window, any spend or market limits); a limit the
terms don't set is never stated (`no-invented-spec`). It turns "you want rights to my
content" into "they've already told me exactly how small this is" (often just before a yes).

## Priced mode - every right its own line (*priced*)

The base fee buys the organic post. Each right we ask for is priced from
`{{USAGE_RIGHTS_RATES}}` (`deals.md`), never from memory or an earlier thread.

- **One line per right, each with its window:** organic repost on our channels, paid usage
  (ads from our account), an ad code (Spark Ads, partnership ads: ads run from their
  handle), raw files. Worded inside the rate sentence (`rights-pricing`); extensions at the
  table's price per 30 days. A right the table doesn't price -> no figure: name it, ask
  their price for that window, ⚠️.
- **An ad code or whitelisting access states its end date:** the start, the window, that
  access ends then (they can revoke it in the platform), and that renewing is a new priced
  line. Access needs their explicit consent.
- **Their rights prices** (rate cards often add a percentage per right): compared line by
  line with the table; above it -> trade the window down (30 days, not 90) before money.
- **They drop a right -> the fee comes down by that line's price.**
- **Buyouts, perpetual or all-media rights, or use beyond social (TV, print, packaging,
  in-store) -> escalate** (Block 0), never priced in a draft. Ask only for rights you'll use.

## Established creators

- **Our paid reach behind your content, at our cost (value lever, established-only).** When
  the flat fee has to come down or go to zero, boosting stops being something we take and
  becomes company-funded paid reach we SPEND behind their content (new creators: retained).
- **G.3 - The ad-run rebalance.** Returning creator pitches more volume but performance
  doesn't justify the rate: decline the next round on performance (`limited-authority`,
  once), then offer a 30-day company-funded ad run on their channel as a no-extra-work
  interim to "rebalance the numbers": two weeks for conversions to mature before a final
  answer, a decline turned into goodwill, no new fee deal struck.
- **Scenario K, the conversion:** an underperforming paid deal can become a free boosting
  arrangement rather than a burned relationship.

## Amplification / whitelisting (reply shape)

**(a) History check before offering.** The rights record is the user's system of record
(the note's commercial snapshot, the CRM, the signed terms). **No recorded rights means no
boosting rights** - not the same as the creator having refused. Prior threads and the note
give **stance and sentiment only**, never the rights record ("sure, sounds fine" in a
thread is not a grant):

- Prior stance against → don't re-pitch; omit it, or acknowledge the hesitation lightly.
- Happy with it → carry it forward.
- No record → a fresh benefit-framed proposal: "helps the videos reach a broader
  audience and drives more conversions on your link too, so it's a win on both sides."

**(b) Offering versus executing.** Offering inside a deal or renewal is fine once (a)
passes; **EXECUTING** a boost, whitelisting a video, or any ad-code workflow waits for the
human's go-ahead. An ad code the creator sends gets thanks and a defer ("I'll be in touch
in the following weeks on the next steps") - **no timing promises**.

<!-- rule:rights-pricing -->
**Pricing rights.** Rights the base fee includes (*included-first-deal*:
`{{BOOSTING_TERMS}}`) are named inside the rate sentence. Anything beyond them - in
*priced* mode, every right - is **its own line**, priced from `deals.md`
(*included-first-deal* with no figure there: about +20% of the base fee per 30 days per
video), never silently bundled (you'd pay twice or never actually get it), and **worded
inside the rate sentence**, never a separate recap bullet: "[fee] per video including
[window] of boosting rights, plus [price] for [extra window] - [total] in all." Content
rates are ORGANIC reach only; a cross-post to another short-form platform is its own line
too, about +15% by default.

## Never (this module)

- *Included-first-deal:* never trade boosting away on a NEW creator ("no benchmark yet").
- *Priced:* never call a right "included", price one from memory, or let an ad code run
  past its end date.
- Never price a buyout or a perpetual right in a draft.
