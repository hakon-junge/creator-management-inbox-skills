# Module: agency-talent (a manager or agency prices for the creator)

On by the pack default or `creator_types: agency-talent`. Mode `prefer-direct` (a soft
decline is the first response) or `engage` (the agency is a normal channel: work with it
from the start). The core's `first-number` points here for a manager. First confirm it IS
a manager (a different name or address, third-person "I represent...", a rate card);
unsure -> treat them as the creator and run the normal playbook.

## I - A manager or agency replies for the creator (high caution)

- **`engage`: our scope, then their quote against it - never a soft decline.** Reply with
  a fully specified scope (deliverables, platforms, post window, each right and
  exclusivity we need with its window, payment terms) and ask for their quote on exactly
  that - never "what's your rate?" on an open scope, never our budget first. Their quote
  on our scope IS a basis: read it line by line (`inbox rate --ask`, the rights and
  exclusivity tables), then counter from the card (OPEN plus each right they asked about,
  priced from its table; windows and lengths before the fee) or pass warmly - never the
  calibration question after a rate card. CC whoever the agent copied (recipients only
  from the thread); never go around the agent.
- **`prefer-direct`:** the default first response is a soft decline - we prefer a direct,
  long-term relationship with the creator ("not a hard rule, so if there's a strong reason
  it makes sense here, I'm open to hearing it").
- **"What's your budget?"** - don't name a number, flip it: "great question - how do you
  calibrate your creator fees to a brand like us?" (median views, niche, results or
  audience quality = professional; a vague "market rate" = extracting). Still demanding
  the budget -> `prefer-direct`: decline gracefully with the direct-relationship line;
  `engage`: restate the scope and ask for their quote on it.
- **Engaging seriously (`prefer-direct`, no quote yet):** be upfront and symmetric -
  confirm we do have a budget set, ask how they price and offer ours once both sides have
  context, then ask for the creator's stats as you would directly. Their commission scales
  with the fee, so our number has to survive their math; never ask them to cut it, or for
  the creator's rate "behind" theirs.
- Their contract paper (rights, exclusivity, kill fee, payment terms) is read against
  `deals.md`; anything beyond what an active module prices -> Block 0.

## Agency / manager creator-recommendation lists (reply shape)

Vet and decide in ONE reply, no later follow-up: read the sheet, then cross-reference every
name against the creator notes (`inbox creator <name>`: prior collabs,
`do-not-re-onboard`) and the `company.md` target and excluded niches. Names you can't
judge from what's on record -> listed for the user's vetting call (⚠️), never guessed.

Declines stay **grouped and high-level** - ICP fit, "already worked with / in touch";
**per-name detail goes into the shared sheet**, colour-coded (yellow = already worked with
or in conversation). Pivot forward with a crisp ICP profile - "creators serving `{{ICP1}}`
and `{{ICP2}}` with real buying intent" - so the next batch self-filters.

A name a colleague owns with a live deal -> route it inside the reply ("[Colleague] is
closer to [X], they'll follow up") and CC that colleague. A deliberate deactivation or an
unvettable name -> draft everything else and ⚠️ that name.

**Big-gap agency quotes: a clean warm pass is fine** when several creators are quoted well
above a first-test range and there's no strategic reason to re-scope: "those numbers are a
good bit above where we can go for a first test collaboration, so we're going to pass for
this round - happy to revisit if there's ever more room on their side." Frame as fit,
stage or budget, **never "overpriced."** In `engage`, a creator on the list who fits gets
the scoped quote request first.

## Never (this module)

- Never put internal profitability, vetting or do-not-re-onboard language in a manager's
  email: it's about your economics, and in a manager's inbox it travels.
- Never layer the affiliate offer onto a decline through a manager - a creator-direct
  lever that reads as a brush-off there.
- `engage`: never a soft decline, and never around the agent to the creator.
