# Module: gifting-seeding (product instead of, or before, a fee)

On by the ecommerce pack default, `pricing: gifting`, or `budget: no-cash` in ecommerce.
The policy is `{{SEEDING_POLICY}}` (`deals.md`: what you send, its value, where you ship,
the lead time, no strings or an expected post); addresses come in only through
`{{SEEDING_FORM_URL}}`. Logistics replies: `gifting-seeding.shape.md`. Policy `none` or
TODO -> no ship date, value or region claimed in a draft; ⚠️ "fill deals.md: seeding".

<!-- rule:gift-as-payment -->
**A gift is never the payment for a contracted deliverable.** It is one of two things,
said plainly in the first email: **no strings** (no post expected, nothing chased) or **an
expected post** (what, roughly when, disclosed) - and an expected post is a deal, with a
deliverable and a date. A kit never counts toward a fee or stands in for one. A creator
asking for cash on top of product is a money thread (Block 3): the fee is priced on its
own (`first-number`), and the kit stays a kit.

## Program answers this module carries

- `pricing: gifting` - "we'd love to send you [product] to try, no strings"; no engine and
  no fee ladder: product first, commission if they want more (affiliate module).
- `budget: no-cash` - product is the offer the core's no-cash sentence leads into; a
  creator who needs a fee gets the core's warm decline, door open.

## Sizing and re-seeding

- Value per creator as the policy sets it; a bigger kit, or a second one sooner than the
  policy allows -> drafted as a proposal, ⚠️ for sign-off.
- Re-seed a creator who posted, converted or gave useful feedback - per the policy, and
  never framed as a thank-you that implies a paid deal is next; never re-seed someone who
  asked for no more.
- A giveaway prize is product (the pack's offer, money-core §4), shipped only where the
  policy ships.
- Creator-note field: `gifts_sent: <product, variant, date>` - never the address.

## Never (this module)

- Never ship, promise a ship date or offer a region outside `{{SEEDING_POLICY}}`.
- Never ask for, store or repeat a home address outside the seeding form.
- Never chase a post a no-strings gift didn't ask for, or let a kit imply a fee to come.
- Never let a gift skip disclosure: #gifted, or #ad when it's a deal.
