# Module: ugc-per-asset (content made for your channels)

On by `creator_types: ugc` or `success: content`. Seed: the rule moved here from the
playbook's program notes; per-asset pricing comes later.

UGC creators make content for your channels, not theirs. Price per asset; audience and views
are irrelevant. Usage rights are the core deliverable, not an add-on. No promo codes or
tracking links. Content review focuses on whether the asset works as an ad.
