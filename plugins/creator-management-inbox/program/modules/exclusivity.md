# Module: exclusivity (a creator not working with competitors for a while)

On by the pack default or `modules: +exclusivity`. Prices come from `{{EXCLUSIVITY_RATES}}`
(`deals.md`: which category, how long, what it costs). With this module off, every
exclusivity ask is Block 0 (the workflow's escalation list); a table that says `none`
means we don't buy it, and an ask for it is Block 0 too.

## When WE ask for it

- **A narrow, named category** - the product type the post sells against ("other mineral
  sunscreens"), never a whole vertical ("beauty", "any brand") and never their personal
  life; a named competitor list only if the table allows one.
- **A length from the table, with an end date in writing**, counted from the post's
  go-live unless the table says otherwise; extensions per 30 days at the table's price.
  Never perpetual, never "while we work together", never open-ended.
- **Priced as its own line, worded inside the rate sentence** like any right
  (`rights-pricing`): "[fee] for the reel, plus [price] for 30 days of category
  exclusivity from go-live - [total] in all." Totals above the limit: `signoff-total`.
- Which channels it covers (organic posts, paid ads, every platform they use) - only what
  the table names, said outright, never implied wider.
- Ask only for what the campaign needs: it blocks part of their income, so they hear it
  as a cost to them - which it is.

## When it's in THEIR terms

- An exclusivity line on their rate card we didn't ask for -> silent omission
  (architecture's rule). A clause in THEIR terms or contract -> one plain line taking it
  out ("we don't buy exclusivity on this booking"): silence on their paper reads as yes.
- Their contract binds US (no other creators in the niche, a minimum spend) -> Block 0,
  contract structure.
- They want more than the table allows (longer, broader, pricier) -> trade the length or
  the category down before the price; past the table -> ⚠️, no figure beyond it.

## They hold exclusivity with another brand

A competitor's hold is temporary, not a no. Never ask them to work around it or breach
it: note `exclusivity_until: <date> (<category>)` and re-approach after that date
(creator-notes). If our brief needs that category inside their window, it's a pass for
this campaign, door open.

## Never (this module)

- Never perpetual, open-ended or whole-vertical exclusivity; never one without an end date.
- Never price it from memory or an old thread - only from `{{EXCLUSIVITY_RATES}}`.
- Never fold it into the fee unnamed: its own line, its own price.
- Never ask a creator to breach another brand's exclusivity.
