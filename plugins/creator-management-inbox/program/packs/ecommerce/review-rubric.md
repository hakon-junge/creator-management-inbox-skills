# Pack rubric: ecommerce - the product-in-use gates

Loads in Block 2 with `content-reviewer` when `business: ecommerce`. The reviewer keeps the
structural gates (native fit, hook, CTA presence, captions, coherence, brand); these are
the gates that depend on selling a physical product. There is no software-tutorial gate:
the body shows the product in someone's real life, not a step-by-step walkthrough.

## Hook (Gate 2)

- Short-form: the product is in frame - in hand or in use, not only a pack shot - within
  the first seconds; long-form brings it in where the creator's own format would.
- The pain point or the wow is one this audience actually has (dry skin in winter, a
  cluttered shelf, a long commute), said the way they say it.

## Body: the product in use, honestly (Gate 3)

- **Used, not held.** On skin, in the kitchen, worn, assembled - the way a buyer would
  use it, in the creator's real setting; texture, fit, size, scent or finish shown or
  told from their own experience.
- **Results shown as they are.** A before/after needs the same light, angle and camera,
  and no filter, retouch or beauty mode on the result; a time-lapse says how long it
  took. A result a typical buyer won't get is a claim (Gate 7).
- **One reason to buy, for this audience:** why this product over what they already
  use (`{{DIFFERENTIATORS}}`) - never a competitor named or shown in a bad light.
- A balanced aside ("it's thick, so a little goes a long way") is a strength, not a fix.

## Packaging, variant and shade accuracy (Gates 5-6)

- The product on screen is the one the brief names: variant, shade, size, scent and
  colourway, in current packaging - a retired pack or old formula is a fix (`company.md`,
  "Brand assets").
- Names exact everywhere - spoken, in on-screen text, captions and the post caption, as
  `company.md` spells them. Shade and variant names are the words auto-captions mangle
  most: check every one.
- Obviously ours: the label or logo readable at least once and the name said aloud; a
  decanted or unbranded product fails the gate.
- A price, bundle or "sold out" line matches the live store for the post window.

## Claims and disclosure (Gate 7)

- **Disclosure matches the deal and is never optional.** Paid -> #ad (or the market's
  equivalent) and the platform's paid-partnership label where it has one; product only
  -> #gifted, or what that market requires - a gift still gets disclosed; commission ->
  disclosed as well. It sits up front: the caption's first line, and on screen or spoken
  in a video - never buried in a hashtag block or behind "more". Missing or wrong =
  Request edits, every time.
- **Claims:** experience language passes; a claim on the pack's regulated list
  (`program/packs/ecommerce/PACK.md`) is Block 0 per `claims-handling` (live: the takedown
  ask); any other claim the brand can't back gets the draft-and-flag reframe ask.
- A child on camera, or a product for children, is on the regulated list.

## CTA (Gate 4)

- The end CTA names the code exactly and what it gets (`{{CREATOR_PROMO_OFFER}}`), said
  and on screen - the code still works when the post is reshared or screenshotted.
- Where to go, by platform: a product tag or shop link where the platform has one, a link
  sticker in stories, the description link on long-form; "link in bio" alone is weak.
- An offer end date said on camera only if it holds for the whole post window.

## The brief's must-haves gate; its suggestions never do

- Must-haves: the hero product and variant, the key message, required shots, the
  disclosure, the code and link, the post window, the do-not-show list.
- Suggestions are the creator's to take or leave; their format and voice win them.
- A length, frame count or format detail gates only when the brief or `deals.md` sets it
  (`no-invented-spec`).

## Content that will run as an ad (usage rights in the deal)

- Licensed audio only: a trending sound cleared for an organic post often isn't cleared
  for ads - flag it before the ad runs, not after.
- Key text and the product stay inside the platforms' safe zones; a clean version with no
  burned-in captions is asked for only when the rights terms include raw files.
