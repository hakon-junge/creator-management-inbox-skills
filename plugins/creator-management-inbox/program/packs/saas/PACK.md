# Pack: saas (software or an app: signups and paid subscriptions)

Chosen by `business: saas`. The creator gets the product - an included plan or a trial;
the audience gets a code or a trial; success is signups and paid subscriptions. Default
modules: affiliate (recurring), views-pricing, budget-always-on, usage-rights
(included-first-deal), agency-talent (prefer-direct). Loads with the negotiation core in
Block 3.

## Success, said to a creator

- `signups`: "What we look at is signups from your audience." The bar (if `deals.md` allows
  sharing it): signups needed. Recaps report clicks and signups.

## The offer: an included plan, never a lever

- Complimentary `{{PLAN_TOP}}` access stays onboarding, never a lever - and even that isn't
  a first-email selling point. Always given, never framed as a concession.
- **An included `{{PLAN_TOP}}` (a value lever on pushback).** Every collaborating creator
  gets the top plan for the duration, maxed on `{{CREDIT_UNIT}}` so they never run out,
  theirs to use for their own business too. Lands hardest with someone who'd actually use
  the product. Framed as included and generous, never as a concession. (Separate from
  onboarding access - see `{{CREATOR_TRIAL_GRANT}}`.)
- A parting can keep the access: in Scenario H's worked case the thread closed on the
  pause-not-break exit with `{{PLAN_TOP}}` access kept.

## Plays

- **Trial-first creators with an unbridgeable gap (Scenario C):** if they want to trial the
  product first but their stated rate is already structurally out of range (implied CPM
  multiples above ceiling), don't wait for the trial - it burns `{{CREDIT_UNIT}}` and weeks
  on a zero-probability deal. Run the decline-by-math now and pivot to a surface that could
  actually work.
- **Giveaway and sponsorship tiers are subscriptions and plans only**, sized deliberately,
  and can be lower than the ask (a creator asked for top-tier access for a giveaway and
  received two annual mid-tier subscriptions instead) - stated positively, zero apology,
  closed with "Would that work for you?".
- **What they get beyond the fee** (money-core's Door A):
  `{{CREATOR_TRIAL_GRANT}}`.
- **"Other brands pay more", the business-model answer:** a high-margin transactional
  product or an affiliate-heavy tool can out-pay a subscription product without our number
  being unfair.
- **In bulk (re-onboarding at scale, advanced rate engine only):** run the engine's `--bulk`
  pass first to verdict the whole batch, triage by verdict (CUT and DECLINE are the urgent
  conversations; KEEP can wait), then drop into the per-creator playbook flow one creator at
  a time - status quo can't be batched.

## The how-we-work block, a worked example

For an always-on, per-creator, sales-measured, views-priced program (the demo's): "We don't
work with set budgets - we run an always-on program and price every first collaboration the
same way, so we can work together long-term rather than one-off. Performance for us means
paying customers, not views or likes, and after each video we share clicks, signups and
customers so we can recalibrate together." A campaign program with a fixed pot says
something different and equally true (`budget-campaign-pot`).
