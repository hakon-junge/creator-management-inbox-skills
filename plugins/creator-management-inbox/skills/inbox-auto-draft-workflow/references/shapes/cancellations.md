# Cancellations

A Block 3 thread. Reads: `{{CANCELLATION_TERMS}}` (`deals.md`: the kill fee by stage, the
notice, what's owed back), what was delivered, paid and sent (the note, the thread), and
the creator's note.

## We cancel

- **Early** - the day it's decided, never on the post date - and **owned**: "this one is
  on us". One honest line of reason (a launch moved, a brief changed); never their
  content or their numbers unless that IS the reason and the data backs it.
- **The kill fee per the terms** for the stage reached, quoted from `deals.md`, never
  rounded or negotiated in the draft; a total above `{{SIGN_OFF_LIMIT}}` ->
  `signoff-total`. Product already sent stays theirs unless the terms say otherwise.
- **Door open:** the next campaign offered only if one exists (`{{CAMPAIGN_CALENDAR}}`);
  otherwise a warm "we'd love to work together when the timing is right".

## They cancel

- **Before delivery, nothing paid:** accept warmly, then state plainly what's owed back
  under the terms - often nothing; product per the terms. No guilt, no "disappointing".
- **Who owes what is read exactly:** a kill fee is usually what the brand pays when the
  BRAND cancels; never ask a creator for money the terms don't name, and correct a
  creator who thinks they owe one, kindly.
- **Before delivery with anything paid upfront, or after payment -> Block 0:** a refund
  is the human's call; at most a warm holding reply that commits nothing.
- **A personal reason** (health, family): empathy first, no questions, door open.

## Either way

- A product or launch delay on our side is a reschedule (`shapes/scheduling.md`), not a
  cancellation.
- Part delivered -> what's owed for that part comes from the terms; nothing in the terms
  -> no figure, ⚠️.
- Never cancel by going silent; never threaten. Record it in the note (the date, who
  cancelled, why, what was settled); a status change is proposed in the report, not made.
