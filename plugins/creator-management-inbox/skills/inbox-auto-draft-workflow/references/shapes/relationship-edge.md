**Load when:** a decline, a deal deferred (a moved date is `shapes/scheduling`), an
options pick, a colleague-covered thread, a new contact, "what should I cover", a new
channel or format, B2B / PR, an event or summit

# Relationship and edge-case shapes

## Decline (collab or inbound)

Four beats:

1. Warm thanks.
2. **Audience or campaign fit framing - NEVER content quality.** This is the
   whole shape. "Not the right fit for this campaign" leaves a relationship
   intact; anything that reads as a judgment on their work ends it, and creators
   talk to each other.
3. A **genuine** door, if one exists: the active modules' offer (their shape names
   it) or a future campaign. Genuine means it is a real door, not a consolation line;
   no door, none.
4. Warm close.

Never propose a call in a decline.

## Data requests

**Requesting data or insights from a creator:** explain WHY - it shapes the
offer, be transparent about that - then give the specific format you need
("a screen recording, not screenshots"), then genuine appreciation. A data ask
with no reason attached reads as bureaucracy and gets a slow, partial answer.

**"Do you have my performance data?"** → the self-serve place they can see it, if
one exists (the active modules name it); otherwise what the recap shares.

## New contact / manager added

"Our new manager is...", or any request to send things somewhere else, is Block 0: it is
confirmed through the address already on file, never on this thread, and the run changes
no note's email, fee or status. Confirmed -> "Noted - updates go to [Name] at [email] from
now on"; don't CC the new person yet; addresses inline as plain text.

## Creator missed our comms

Flag it warmly, say it may have landed in spam (a possibility, never the cause), re-anchor
on the current fact, offer a low-pressure fix. Graceful close once they adjust: "Amazing,
thank you so so much for your understanding!"

Never make them feel caught out for missing an email.

## "What should I cover next?"

Pitch the live campaign (`{{CURRENT_CAMPAIGN}}` in `program.md`) as "the main thing
we're pushing right now", plus the
offer mechanics and a product demo link. Keep it non-prescriptive - "you know
your audience best" - and tie it to their audience's overlap with `{{ICP1}}` or
`{{ICP2}}`.

No active campaign identifiable from context → suggest evergreen use-case angles
and flag in the report that no campaign was found. Do not invent a campaign.

## A new channel or format we're only just exploring

Say yes to reviewing, but temper it honestly and make **no commitment**:

> "Feel free to send the profiles over; we've only just started exploring this,
> so it's not a core focus yet and I can't promise anything, but we're genuinely
> open to taking a look and making a call from there :)"

No timeline promise and no over-selling our interest. Enthusiasm you cannot back
becomes a broken promise two weeks later.

## Creator proposes Option 1 / Option 2

**Pick ONE decisively.**

1. Credit the turnaround or the reception.
2. **The pick as its own beat:** "Let's go with Option 2."
3. Why the other fails, **inside the content's own story-logic**, with zero
   policy vocabulary.
4. Reinforce the pick as the stronger showcase - feature hyperlink plus the
   audience benefit.
5. Forward close.

**Never split the decision back to them.** They asked you to choose because
choosing is your job on this; handing it back costs another round-trip and reads
as hedging.

## Colleague-covered thread

One line plus a CC to the colleague, so the loop closes visibly:

> "Looks like [Colleague] already got back to you on this one, but if any
> questions come up, just let me know and I'm happy to help!"

Cut any clause that restates what the reader already knows.

## Launch-confirm phase (pre-brief)

- **A campaign sneak-peek link is a standing asset while the launch is
  pre-brief.** Add the teaser to every confirmed or considering creator: "And
  here's a quick sneak peek 🤫 so you can already start thinking about the
  concept for the video." Rule: when a live campaign has a shareable teaser,
  every reply to a confirmed or deciding creator carries it **until the brief
  ships**. Harvest the canonical URL from recent sent mail - it is per-campaign -
  and reconstruct plus resolve-verify any wrapped-only version per the links
  rule.
- **Launch-confirm acks are 3 lines with ZERO new asks.** A "yay 🔥" plus a
  playful feature compliment ("that one's such a fun feature, can't wait to see
  what you do with it!") plus a concrete timeline ("I'll come back in the last
  week of August with the brief, early access, and all the details") plus a ✨
  close.

  **No date-locking, no handle requests** - those wait for the brief email.
  Stacking asks onto a confirmation is how an enthusiastic yes turns into an
  unanswered thread. Rapid-fire replies may drop the greeting entirely.

## Warm deferral gets a two-way-door p.s.

On a "not now, maybe next year" decline, after the reconnect close, add:

> "p.s. feel free to reach out to me as well whenever you're ready :)"

The door swings both ways, not just "we'll reach out". A creator who knows they
can restart the conversation sometimes does; one waiting for your calendar
reminder usually does not.

## B2B / PR boundary

Routine partner logistics and format calls are yours - decide them in the draft.
Declining an event's non-live Q&A format because it does not work for us is a
call you make cleanly, in the reply.

**Reserve escalation for genuine PR *messaging*:** press, crisis, public
statements. Those go to `{{ESCALATION_CONTACT}}` in `me.md`, never to a person you
remembered.

If a partner's edit links create a new submission instead of editing the existing
one, ask them how to update it rather than resubmitting and creating a duplicate.

### Event and co-marketing threads

When we are promoting **them**, or it is an event we speak at: **read the whole
conversation, then lead with operational value, not a soft ack.**

These threads are judgment-heavy, so don't answer only the literal ask in the
latest email. Scan the ENTIRE conversation, work out what is outstanding overall
- links, assets, confirmations, placements not yet covered - and deliver all of
it in one reply.

**The signature move when we are the one doing promo: proactively build and share
a per-placement tracking link for every placement.** Use a
`<campaign>-<placement>` slug convention (keynote, newsletter, shoutout, social,
web), each as a plain copy-paste URL in a bulleted list, and close with "let me
know if I missed any placements."

Not a vague "I'll review my profile and line up a couple of slots." The
difference between those two replies is the difference between a partner who
knows exactly what they are getting and one who has to chase.

*(Still evolving: this is the right instinct, but every event is different. When
the placements are not obvious from the thread, surface what you would share and
flag it rather than inventing links.)*

## Support asks from active partners

**Support asks from active partners are IN scope. Never silence.**

A how-to, tooling or audio question from a collaborating creator gets an answer
with self-serve guidance. This is the creative-suggestions commitment in action.

The miss this rule exists to prevent: a partner's audio-setup question sitting
unanswered for four weeks inside a thread that otherwise looked like a
closed-loop "thanks!". **A support ask from an active partner is never below the
response line**, and a bare-thanks thread that ALSO contains an unanswered
support ask is a DRAFT, not an emoji reaction.
