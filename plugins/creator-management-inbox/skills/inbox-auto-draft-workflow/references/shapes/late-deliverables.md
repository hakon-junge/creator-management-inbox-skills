**Load when:** a contracted draft or post is past its agreed date and the creator hasn't
moved it themselves

# Late deliverables - chasing what we're owed

A creator who moves a date gets `shapes/scheduling.md`; a quiet non-money ask gets its one
bump (`follow-up-ladder`). This shape is for a CONTRACTED deliverable past its date. Reads:
the agreed and original dates (note, thread), `deals.md`'s late-post and cancellation
terms, the campaign window (`{{CURRENT_CAMPAIGN}}`), anything paid upfront.

## The chase

1. **Day +1 - a friendly status ask that names the date.** "Just checking in on the video
   we had down for the 14th - how's it coming along?" No guilt, no "as agreed".
2. **Day +5 - pin a date, with the real reason.** The reason is something true on our
   side: a code that's live only until a date, the campaign window, a launch the post
   was timed around. "Could we lock in a day this week? The code runs until the 30th, so
   anything after that loses its offer." One ask, two concrete days to pick from.
3. **Still nothing after day +5:** no third chase. It's a decision now, below.

## The decision (Block 3)

Past the window, or weeks late: a terms decision for Block 3, where the bound budget
module's late-delivery rule decides (a campaign thread converts or passes; always-on runs
the performance x communication gate). A conversion (next campaign, swapped deliverable,
partial fee) is one offer, ⚠️ in the report; a proposed date past the window gets
in-window dates plus that one fallback, in the same email.

- **Paid upfront and silent 14+ days past the date -> Block 0:** recovering money is the
  human's call; no draft asks for it back.
- **The slip is ours** (product, a brief or an approval arrived late): say so plainly,
  move the date, no chase.

## Every time

- Record each slip against the ORIGINAL date in the note (`creator-notes`): "due Oct 3 ->
  posted Oct 12, chased twice". Reliability is measured from the first date, never the
  latest one.
- Never threaten - no kill fee, contract or "we'll have to cancel" in a chase. A
  cancellation is its own email (`shapes/cancellations.md`), after the decision.
- Never invent a deadline or a consequence `deals.md` doesn't state.
