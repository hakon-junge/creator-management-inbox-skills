## Call mode (only when a call actually happens)

Email is the default channel for every money conversation; whether a call happens at all is
`comms-style` (calls) and the user's `me.md`. Never move a hard decision to a call to avoid
writing it, and in a decline or parting stay async even when they ask - a call there just
re-litigates the no.

When a call does happen, the async toolkit mostly transfers; these are the call-only mechanics:

- **Bare mirrors work out loud.** Repeating their last one to three words reads as sarcasm in
  writing but as engagement in speech - use freely to draw them out and to buy thinking time
  when surprised.

- **Slow, downward-inflected delivery for the hard numbers.** The calm-and-certain voice for
  the recalibrated fee or the hold; save the playful voice for rapport and the tense-moment
  defuse. Never match an assertive's heat - de-escalate, they reset fast.

- **Six actual seconds of silence after the key line or number.** Count it. The other side
  fills the void, and what they fill it with is the information you need. With analysts the
  silence itself bonds.

- **"That's right" before any number.** Summarize their position - laid on thick - until they
  confirm it, THEN the shape. A "you're right" means don't advance yet.

- **Playfulness disarms the money moment.** A self-deprecating beat before the hard number
  ("you're going to want to throw something at me for this one") lands better than a defensive
  wind-up - it's the accusation audit wearing a smile.

- **Type-read from their first substantive answer,** then pick tools: analyst = math + time +
  silence; accommodator = let them talk, pin every specific before hanging up; assertive =
  summarize them first, be crisp, respect before playfulness.

- **Close the call by pinning contact predictability:** what happens next and exactly when
  they'll hear from us.

- **Capture the call** in `{{CALL_RECORDER}}` where the tooling allows it, and write the
  agreed terms back into email the same day. A call that is not written up did not happen -
  the email record is the contract trail, not the transcript.
