## Your program in your own words

`{{PROGRAM_PITCH}}`: We run four product campaigns a year, each with a set budget and 10-12 paid creators, and between campaigns we seed our PR box to new creators with no strings attached and keep a small bench of UGC creators making ads for us. Paid creators get a one-off booking for the campaign with their own discount code; the ones whose codes sell get booked again for the next campaign. We share every post's orders with the creator after the campaign closes.

## Anything else about how you work

- Influencers and agencies quote their own price; we read it against `rates.md`.
  UGC creators get our rate card instead: we name the per-video fee.
- Product first: most paid creators were gifted a PR box before we booked them.
- Every paid post goes through approval before it goes live (`deals.md`).
- We never pay upfront, and we never pay before a post is live and approved.
