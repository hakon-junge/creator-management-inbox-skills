## Claims and disclosure (skincare is regulated - read before any content review)

- **Cosmetics, not medicines.** Never a claim to treat, cure, heal, clear or prevent a
  condition: acne, breakouts, eczema, rosacea, dermatitis, psoriasis, "cleared my skin".
  In the US that makes the product an unapproved drug (FDA); in the UK and EU it is a
  medicinal claim a cosmetic may not make (Cosmetics Regulation; ASA/CAP code in the UK).
- **No timed or guaranteed results**: "in 3 days", "overnight", "100% of people". No
  before/after shots with filters or different lighting.
- **SPF**: say the label exactly ("Broad Spectrum SPF 30" in the US, "SPF 30" in the
  UK/EU). Never "sunblock", "waterproof", "sweatproof", "all-day protection" or "you
  don't need sunscreen" - the tint is not tested for water resistance.
- **"Clean"** means our published Clean Standard (https://quillmoss.example/clean). Never
  "chemical-free", "non-toxic", "safe in pregnancy", "hypoallergenic" or "dermatologist
  approved" (not tested).
- **Approved claims** creators may use: "supports a healthy-looking skin barrier", "skin
  feels softer and more hydrated", "helps skin look more even", "fragrance-free", "vegan
  and cruelty-free".
- **Disclosure on every paid or code post**: the platform's paid-partnership label AND
  "#ad" at the start of the caption (US FTC, UK ASA, Ireland CCPC). Gifted-only posts:
  "#gifted" or "PR product". A post with a discount code is an ad even when unpaid,
  because the code earns commission.
- A regulated claim on a live post is a same-day issue: see `me.md` (regulatory lead).
