## How you use it in negotiations

- Every paid creator's campaign code is also their Collabs code: it earns 15% on top
  of the fee, until the code stops when the campaign window closes.
- Gifted creators are invited to Collabs; it's never a condition of the PR box.
- UGC creators don't get codes (their videos run from our ad accounts).
- Never promise commission "for life" or any permanence these terms don't state.
