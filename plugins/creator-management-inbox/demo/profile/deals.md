# How you do deals with creators (DEMO - fictional)

- Formats: YouTube integrations and dedicated videos, TikTok videos, Instagram Reels
- `{{MIN_PIECES_FIRST_DEAL}}`: 2
- `{{TRACK_GATE_MEDIAN_VIEWS}}`: 8000
- `{{CASH_FLOOR_FIRST_DEAL}}`: 300 USD
- `{{PAYOUT_VENDOR}}`: PayPal
- Payment terms: paid within 14 days of the video going live and being submitted
- `{{PAYMENT_SPEED_CLAIM}}`: within 14 days of go-live
- Gross or net of tax: gross - agreed fees include any VAT or sales tax
- Upfront payment: never on a first deal
- `{{BOOSTING_TERMS}}`: 30 days of boosting rights included on request - the original video only, run from the creator's own handle, no re-cuts or separate ad versions; extensions at 20% of the base fee per 30 days per video
- `{{CONVERSION_VALUE}}`: about 45 USD per paid customer (internal only)
- `{{PAYBACK_WINDOW}}`: 90 days
- What counts as a win: paid Pro customers from the creator's link or code
- May you tell a creator their conversion bar: yes, as a customer count only
- After a yes: send the onboarding form; no separate contract for standard deals
- Giveaways: up to 10 Pro codes (6 months each); always flagged for sign-off
